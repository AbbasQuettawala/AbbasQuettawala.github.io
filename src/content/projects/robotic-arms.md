## The challenge

Build a set of small, approachable robotic arms that people can interact with at a showcase. The project needed mechanical construction, electronics and an interface that made the motion easy to discover.

## My part

I collaboratively designed and built ten working six-degree-of-freedom arms. We adapted a FABRI CREATOR design, creating custom 3D-printed bases with the Monash Automation logo and integrating ESP32 control, servos, LiPo power and touchscreens.

We also developed a custom control protocol with emotes that users could trigger from the touchscreen. Both the hardware and protocol work were collaborative.

## From touch to movement

1. A visitor selects an emote on the touchscreen.
2. The custom control protocol represents that selection.
3. The arm executes the corresponding motion through its servo control.

## Progress & evidence

We demonstrated the arms to senior engineering executives at the Engineering Dean’s Lunch showcase. The project connected hands-on fabrication and integration with the practical experience of showing a robot to someone outside the team.
